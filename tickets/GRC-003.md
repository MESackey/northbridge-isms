# GRC-003 · Where is our care data?
Raised by: Grace Whitfield (DPO) · Opened: Mon 9 Nov 2026 08:00 · Due: Sat 14 Nov 12:00 · Priority: P2
Status: In progress · Closed: —

## Request (in one sentence)
Grace wants me to map where care data goes end to end, from an agency entering a care plan to the data being deleted, including every system it touches inside AWS and outside it.

## Plan (written Fri 13 Nov)
1. Build `01-company/data-inventory.csv` mapping every category of care data, its classification, storage locations, and lifecycles based on interview notes and system records.
2. Create `01-company/data-flow-care-record.md` diagramming the care record's end-to-end journey (create → use → share → store → back up → delete) with trust boundaries marked.
3. Identify and document at least four risky data flows as candidate risks ("Flows that worry me") with encryption status confirmed, assumed, or unknown.
4. Update weekly status note and review findings against interview notes and open questions.
5. Finalize ticket paperwork, time logs, and close ticket by Sat 14 Nov 12:00.
