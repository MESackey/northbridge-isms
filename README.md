# northbridge-isms

Governance records from a **90-day GRC job simulation** (self-directed project, 26 Oct 2026 to 23 Jan 2027).

I hired myself as Junior GRC Analyst at **Northbridge Health Ltd**, a fictional UK health-tech SaaS company
that hosts care records for ~41,000 people receiving home care. Every week I worked one realistic ticket
drawn from five real entry-level GRC job ads, and closed it with the paperwork a real employer would expect.

The company is fictional. The work, methods and frameworks are real. The technical environment lives in
[northbridge-grc](https://github.com/MESackey/northbridge-grc) (AWS, Terraform, CI scanning), and the risks
here link to evidence there.

## Start here (for reviewers)

1. `08-board/executive-summary-2027-01.md`: the one-page board summary
2. `03-risk/risk-register.md`: the scored, owned, mapped risk register
3. `07-incidents/INC-2026-001/incident-report.md`: a timed vendor-breach response
4. `09-review/day-90-review.md`: what I delivered, learned, and would do differently

## Frameworks

NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 (risk assessment) · ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS, Annex A) · NIST CSF 2.0 ·
UK GDPR / DPA 2018 · NHS DSPT · Cyber Essentials Plus · SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (mapping) · FAIR (top risks)
