# My GRC glossary

Add every new term the day you meet it: the word, what it means in plain English, and where you met it.

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Where I met it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GRC | Governance, Risk and Compliance: deciding how the company is run safely (governance), finding and scoring what could go wrong (risk), and proving it follows the rules it must (compliance) | Day 1 |
| ISMS | Information Security Management System: the policies, registers and records a company uses to run security. ISO 27001 auditors check it | Day 1 |
| Repository (repo) | A project folder that Git tracks, keeping a dated history of every change | Day 1 |
| Commit | One saved snapshot of changes in a repo, with a message saying what changed | Day 1 |
| Push | Sending your commits from your laptop up to GitHub | Day 1 |
| Ticket | A logged request for work, with an owner, a deadline and a record of what was done | Day 1 |
