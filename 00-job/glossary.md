# My GRC glossary

Add every new term the day you meet it: the word, what it means in plain English, and where you met it.

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Where I met it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GRC | Governance, Risk and Compliance: deciding how the company is run safely (governance), finding and scoring what could go wrong (risk), and proving it follows the rules it must (compliance) | Day 1 |
| ISMS | Information Security Management System: the policies, registers and records a company uses to run security. ISO 27001 auditors check it | Day 1 |
| Repository (repo) | A project folder that Git tracks, keeping a dated history of every change | Day 1 |
| Commit | One saved snapshot of changes in a repo, with a message saying what changed | Day 1 |
| Push | Sending your commits from your laptop up to GitHub | Day 1 |
| Ticket | A logged request for work, with an owner, a deadline and a record of what was done | Day 1 |
| DPIA | Data Protection Impact Assessment: A formal written process used to identify and minimise privacy that involves high-risk processing of personal data (e.g. location tracking) | GRC-001 |
| Power/ Interest grid | A stakeholder mapping tool that categorises people based on how much power they have to affect your work (High/ Low) and how interested they are in it (High/ Low) to determine engagement strategy | GRC-001 |
| Sub processor | A third-party supplier that a data processor (like Northbridge) passes personal data to; under UK GDPR, a processor needs the controller's permission before using one | GRC-003 |
| Data minimisation | A UK GDPR principle requiring that personal data collected, shared, and retained must be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which they are processed. | GRC-003 |
