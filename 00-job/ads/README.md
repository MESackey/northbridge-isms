## What this shows

- Entry-level GRC ads close fast: four of the five original links had gone within weeks.
- Most "junior" ads still ask for 1-2 years' experience, so a portfolio of real GRC work matters.
- The same core duties appear across the ads: ISO 27001 support, risk assessments and registers,
  supplier assurance, audit evidence, policies and reporting.
- Lesson: capture evidence at the time, with the date and web address on it.
